Privacy Policy

CCC Admin Inventory Sync · Effective date: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the CCC Admin Inventory Sync application (the "App"), operated by Canadian Cellular Communications ("we", "us"), handles data. The App is a private integration used only by our own business; it is not distributed to other merchants.

1. What data the App accesses

Through the Clover platform, the App reads the following data from our own merchant account:

  • Orders and line items (including notes containing product serial numbers)
  • Payment records associated with those orders
  • Inventory, merchant, employee, and customer records as needed for sales matching

2. How the data is used

Data is used solely to synchronize sales with our internal inventory system: marking serialized products as sold, updating stock quantities, and recording sales for our own bookkeeping. It is also used to initiate card payments on our own Clover devices.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties.
  • We do not use data for advertising or marketing analytics.
  • We do not access data of any merchant other than our own business.

4. Storage and security

Synchronized records are stored in our internal administration system, hosted on secured infrastructure with access limited to authorized employees. API credentials are stored server-side and are never exposed to browsers or third parties. All communication with the Clover platform occurs over HTTPS.

5. Data retention

Sales and inventory records are retained as required for business accounting and warranty tracking, and for as long as required by applicable Canadian law.

6. Customer rights

Customers of Canadian Cellular Communications may contact us to request access to or deletion of their personal information, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this URL.
